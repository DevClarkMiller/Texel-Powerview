# Product Vision

# Product Name

Texel4Trading PowerView

# Vision Statement

PowerView aims to become the leading independent monitoring and analytics platform for distributed solar energy systems.

# Target Group

**Texel Power Company**: Needs to have data on production of power.
**Texel4Trading**: Wants full access to all data produced by the monitoring devices, alerts if panels aren't working so that they can be serviced.
**Consumer**: (customers who buy panel from Texel4Trading): Needs to know if their panels are working, how much power is being generated.

# Needs

-   **Texel power company**, **Texel4Trading**, and **Individual Solar Panel Owners** need reliable and centralized access to power generation data across all inverter systems.
-   They require a unified monitoring solution that consolidates data from different inverter brands into a single platform with consistent formatting and reporting.
-   They need long-term access to both real-time and historical production data, independent of third-party manufacturer apps.
-   They require early detection of performance issues or system failures to prevent unnoticed downtime and revenue loss.
-   They need a future-proof solution that ensures continued visibility and control even if inverter manufacturers discontinue app support or change their platforms.

# Product

-   An embedded device which monitors AC output of an inverter.
-   An analytics platform to monitor metrics read by device.

# Value

-   Long-term access to consistent production data.
-   Reduced risk of undetected system failures which bleeds money from the system owner.
-   Independence from third-party manufacturer apps.

# Business Goals

-   Reduce dependency on third-party applications for monitoring solar systems.
-   Create a scalable monitoring infrastructure that can be offered as a recurring service.

# What This Product is NOT

-   A monitoring device for individual solar panels.
-   A device capable of controlling an invertor (read-only).
-   A replacement inverter or energy management system.
